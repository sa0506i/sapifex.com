Anyvoc

Build a personal vocabulary from anything you read.

Give Anyvoc an article, a photo of a book page or a note you typed. It picks out the words worth learning, translates them, adds an example sentence and tags each one with its CEFR level. A spaced-repetition trainer then drills them until they stick.

In short

  • Your own material. Articles, photos of a page or your own notes; the camera reads printed text for you.
  • Twelve learning languages, an interface in five, and everything except adding new content works offline.
  • A trainer that schedules itself. Leitner boxes decide what is due; quiz modes, listening practice and two games keep it from going stale.
  • Stays on your device. No cloud sync, no ads, no trackers, no analytics. Basic is free and stays free.
